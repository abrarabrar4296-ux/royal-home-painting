📊 Connect Google Sheets

⚠️ Why Google Sheets shows "Could not fetch url: http://localhost:3000..."
Google Sheets runs on Google's cloud servers, which cannot reach private localhost URLs on your computer. When deployed to a public domain, =IMPORTDATA() will sync live.
1️⃣ Direct Import to Google Sheets (Instant) Works on Localhost

Download all current leads right now and import them into Google Sheets in 10 seconds:

📄 Download CSV 📥 Export Excel (.xlsx) Open sheets.new ↗
In Google Sheets: Click File > Import > Upload and choose the downloaded file.
2️⃣ Real-Time Auto Sync (Google Apps Script) Recommended

Pushes every new lead directly into your Google Sheet under royalhomepainting11@gmail.com in real-time. Works 100% reliably on localhost!

1. Open sheets.new > Click Extensions > Apps Script.
2. Paste the code from google-apps-script.js in this project.
3. Click Deploy > New deployment > Web app (Access: Anyone).
4. Paste the Web App URL into your .env file under GOOGLE_SHEET_WEBHOOK_URL=....
3️⃣ Google Sheets =IMPORTDATA Formula (For Live Public Domain)

Once hosted on a public domain (or via npx localtunnel --port 3000), paste this into cell A1 of Google Sheets:

=IMPORTDATA(window.location.origin + "/api/leads.csv")
