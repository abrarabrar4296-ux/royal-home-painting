House Painting

Comprehensive interior and exterior painting engineered for durability, rich color depth, and moisture resistance across Bangalore’s unique climate.

What's Included:
  • Detailed wall crack repair, surface putty & dustless machine sanding
  • Moisture-barrier primer application for long-lasting adhesion
  • 2 to 3 rich coats of premium washable emulsion (Asian Paints / Dulux / Berger)
  • Complete masking of floors, electrical fittings, and furniture
  • Thorough post-job vacuuming & spotless clean handover
  • 1-Year Workmanship Warranty

Terrace Waterproofing

End-to-end roof and terrace waterproofing systems designed to stop standing water leaks, damp ceiling patches, and structural concrete damage permanently.

What's Included:
  • High-pressure jet wash, loose debris removal & expansion crack sealing
  • Multi-layer elastomeric polymer waterproof membrane barrier
  • UV-resistant heat-reflective topcoat (reduces indoor heat by 4–5°C)
  • Parapet wall joint reinforcement & rainwater drain sealing
  • Ponding water test inspection before handover
  • Up to 5-Year Leak-Free Warranty
